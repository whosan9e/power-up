Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 November 2026

Train9 is an independent fitness app made in Sydney, Australia. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the control you have over it.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address, used to sign you in with a one-time code.
  • Profile answers: sex, age, height, weight, goal and physique style, used to set your training plan and calorie and protein targets.
  • Training and food logs: sets, reps, weights, notes, tendon pain scores, foods, macros and body weight entries.
  • AI usage counts: how many AI requests you make each day, so everyone gets a fair daily limit.
  • Ranked stats: your display name, rank, level and season points, which other signed-in players can see on the leaderboard.
  • Notification settings: if you turn on notifications, a push token for your device and your reminder time and time zone.
  • Invite codes: if you join with a friend's code, we record which code you used so they can see you joined.

What we don't collect

  • We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.
  • Meal photos and coach messages are not stored on our servers. When you scan a meal or ask the coach, the request passes through our server to Anthropic's AI to produce the answer, then it's discarded. Anthropic processes it under its commercial terms.
  • We don't use third-party analytics or tracking.

Where your data is stored

Your account and logs are stored with Supabase in its Sydney (ap-southeast-2) region, with access limited so only you can read your own logs. The app itself is hosted on GitHub Pages. A copy of your data is also kept on your phone so the app works offline.

Emails and notifications

We send sign-in codes to your email. Training reminders, new season alerts and invite nudges are sent only if you turn on notifications. Update emails about seasons, events and offers are sent only if you opt in, and you can switch them off at any time in Settings.

Health information

Body weight, food and pain logs can be sensitive. We use them only to run your plan, your stats and your ranked progress inside the app. Train9 gives general fitness guidance and is not medical advice. See a doctor or physio for pain, injury or health concerns.

Your choices

  • Delete your account: Settings, Account, Delete. This permanently removes your account, logs, leaderboard entry and notification tokens from our servers.
  • Export a backup: Settings, Data, Save backup file.
  • Turn off notifications or emails in Settings at any time.

Age

Train9 is intended for people aged 13 and over. Users under 18 should use it with a parent's or guardian's knowledge.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. For privacy questions or requests, contact the developer through github.com/whosan9e/power-up.
